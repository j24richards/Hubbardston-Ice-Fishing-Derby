Winner by weight

  1. 01Anglers 16 and up need a valid Massachusetts freshwater fishing license — bring it with you to check-in.
  2. 02Each angler may fish up to five lines total, in any combination of jigging rods and tip-ups, per state regulations.
  3. 03Fish must be caught on Bent/Sawyer Pond, during derby hours, to qualify — no fish from other waters or other days.
  4. 04All entries are weighed live at the single official scale in the middle of the pond — look for the Bass American flag. No outside scales or self-reported weights, and dead fish will not be accepted; bring an extra bucket to keep your catch swimming until weigh-in.
  5. 05Ties go to whichever fish was weighed in first. Hubbardston Rod & Gun Club rulings on eligibility are final.
  6. 06Ice conditions are each angler's own responsibility. We recommend a minimum of 4" of solid, clear ice before heading out, and spud bars/cleats are strongly encouraged.
  7. 07If the ice isn't safe on derby morning, we'll move to the posted rain/thaw date rather than cancel outright.
  8. 08No holes may be cut before 7:00 AM.
  9. 09Minimum length to weigh in: 14" for bass, 16" for pickerel, 8" for perch — measured tip of the mouth to tip of the tail. All state regulations must be followed.
  10. 10Final weigh-in cutoff is 2:30 PM. If you're not in the weigh-in line by then, your fish can't be weighed.
  11. 11Please respect the pond: carry in, carry out, and leave no trash behind. No fires or motorized vehicles are permitted on the ice.

Sign-Up & Raffles

Pre-register at the club bar anytime before derby day to skip the line, or register on the ice starting at 5:30 AM — registration and raffle ticket sales are at the tents by the entrance, next to the porta-potty.

Bring

Auger, skimmer, bucket or sled, ice cleats, warm layers — and a two-way radio tuned to Channel 5 for derby updates and to reach the weigh station. A few loaner augers on hand for kids' entries.

Restrooms

Porta-potty at the entrance onto the ice, right by the registration tents.

Weather policy

Unsafe ice moves the derby to the posted rain/thaw date — check this page or our email list that morning.

Good To Know

FAQ

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes — anyone 16 or older needs a current Massachusetts freshwater fishing license. Bring a copy (paper or digital) to check-in.

What happens if the ice isn't safe?

We check the ice in the days before the derby. If conditions aren't safe on the day, we move the event to our posted rain/thaw date rather than fish on bad ice.

Can I fish from a shanty or shelter?

Yes, portable shanties are welcome within the marked derby boundary. Please keep walkways clear for the safety sweep.

I didn't catch anything — was it still worth it?

Plenty of anglers go home empty-handed and still make it back next year for the chili.

Where does my entry fee go?

After prizes and event costs, proceeds support the Hubbardston Rod & Gun Club's youth programs and pond stocking.
