Winner by weight

  1. 01Each angler may fish up to five lines total, in any combination of jigging rods and tip-ups, per state regulations.
  2. 02Fish must be caught on Bent/Sawyer Pond, during derby hours, to qualify — no fish from other waters or other days.
  3. 03All entries are weighed live at the single official scale in the middle of the pond — look for the Bass American flag. No outside scales or self-reported weights, and dead fish will not be accepted; bring an extra bucket to keep your catch swimming until weigh-in.
  4. 04Ties go to whichever fish was weighed in first. Hubbardston Rod & Gun Club rulings on eligibility are final.
  5. 05Ice conditions are each angler's own responsibility. We recommend a minimum of 4" of solid, clear ice before heading out, and spud bars/cleats are strongly encouraged.
  6. 06If the ice isn't safe on derby morning, we'll move to the posted rain/thaw date rather than cancel outright.
  7. 07No holes may be cut before 7:00 AM.
  8. 08Minimum length to weigh in: 14" for bass, 16" for pickerel, 8" for perch — measured tip of the mouth to tip of the tail. All state regulations must be followed.
  9. 09Final weigh-in cutoff is 2:30 PM. If you're not in the weigh-in line by then, your fish can't be weighed.
  10. 10Please respect the pond: carry in, carry out, and leave no trash behind. No fires or motorized vehicles are permitted on the ice.

Sign-Up & Raffles

Pre-register at the club bar anytime before derby day to skip the line, or register on the ice starting at 5:30 AM — registration and raffle ticket sales are at the tents by the entrance, next to the porta-potty.

Bring

Auger, skimmer, bucket or sled, ice cleats, warm layers — and a two-way radio tuned to Channel 5 for derby updates and to reach the weigh station. A few loaner augers on hand for kids' entries.

Restrooms

Porta-potty at the entrance onto the ice, right by the registration tents.

Weather policy

Unsafe ice moves the derby to the posted rain/thaw date — check this page or our email list that morning.

Good To Know

FAQ

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes — anyone 16 or older needs a current Massachusetts freshwater fishing license. It's each angler's own responsibility to be properly licensed before fishing.

What happens if the ice isn't safe?

We check the ice in the days before the derby. If conditions aren't safe on the day, we move the event to our posted rain/thaw date rather than fish on bad ice.

Can I fish from a shanty or shelter?

Yes, portable shanties are welcome within the marked derby boundary. Please keep walkways clear for the safety sweep.

Where does my entry fee go?

After prizes and event costs, proceeds support the Hubbardston Rod & Gun Club's youth programs and pond stocking.

Which calendar link should I use?

"+ Google Cal" opens Google Calendar directly and only works if that's what you use. "+ Any Calendar" downloads a standard .ics file instead — tap it once it's downloaded (or the download notification) and your phone will offer to open it in whatever calendar app you actually have, Apple Calendar, Outlook, Samsung Calendar, or otherwise.

How do I get a phone notification when a new fish is logged?

Tap the 🔔 Notify me about new catches button in the Live Leaderboard section above, then allow notifications when your phone asks. The steps differ slightly by phone — iPhone owners, set this up a day or two before the derby so you're not fumbling with it on the ice.

Android

  1. 1Tap 🔔 Notify me about new catches in the Live Leaderboard section.
  2. 2Tap Allow on the prompt that pops up. That's it — you're all set.

iPhone & iPad

⏰ Do this at home before derby day. It only takes a minute, and it's a lot easier with warm hands than on the ice.

iPhones only allow site notifications after the page is added to your Home Screen — a one-time setup:

  1. 1Open this site in the Safari app (the blue compass icon). Other browsers like Chrome can't do this step on iPhone.
  2. 2Tap the Share icon in the toolbar — a square with an arrow pointing up ⬆️. On iPhone it's usually at the bottom of the screen; on iPad, top-right.
  3. 3In the menu that pops up, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4Tap Add in the top-right corner to confirm.
  5. 5Find the new Derby icon on your Home Screen (it looks like the site's icon, not the Safari compass) and open the site from there — not from Safari.
  6. 6Tap 🔔 Notify me about new catches, then tap Allow when asked. Done — you'll now get catch alerts even with the app closed.
