  1. 01Anglers 16 and up need a valid Massachusetts freshwater fishing license — bring it with you to check-in.
  2. 02Each angler may fish up to five lines total, in any combination of jigging rods and tip-ups, per state regulations.
  3. 03Fish must be caught on Comet Pond, during derby hours, to qualify — no fish from other waters or other days.
  4. 04All entries are weighed at the official scale near the warming hut. No outside scales or self-reported weights.
  5. 05Ties go to whichever fish was weighed in first. Committee rulings on eligibility are final.
  6. 06Ice conditions are each angler's own responsibility. We recommend a minimum of 4" of solid, clear ice before heading out, and spud bars/cleats are strongly encouraged.
  7. 07If the ice isn't safe on derby morning, we'll move to the posted rain/thaw date rather than cancel outright.

Good To Know

FAQ

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes — anyone 16 or older needs a current Massachusetts freshwater fishing license. Bring a copy (paper or digital) to check-in.

What happens if the ice isn't safe?

We check the ice in the days before the derby. If conditions aren't safe on the day, we move the event to our posted rain/thaw date rather than fish on bad ice.

Can I fish from a shanty or shelter?

Yes, portable shanties are welcome within the marked derby boundary. Please keep walkways clear for the safety sweep.

I didn't catch anything — was it still worth it?

Plenty of anglers go home empty-handed and still make it back next year for the chili. Entry includes the Mystery Weight jackpot draw either way.

Where does my entry fee go?

After prizes and event costs, proceeds support pond stocking and youth fishing programs in Hubbardston.
